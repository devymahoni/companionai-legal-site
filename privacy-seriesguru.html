SeriesGuru Privacy Policy

This policy applies to SeriesGuru and related quiz products published by Ronaillabs, including store listings such as Short Video Drama Quiz where applicable.

SeriesGuru is an independent entertainment quiz app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or officially connected to any TV network, streaming platform, production company, or rights holder.

1. Summary

The app lets users answer quiz packs, earn scores, unlock levels, view progress, and participate in leaderboard-style features. Depending on the version, the app may include sign-in, ads, analytics, crash diagnostics, and third-party media or metadata used for quiz context.

2. Data we handle

2.1 Account and profile data

  • Email address, display name, profile photo, user identifier, and sign-in provider when a user signs in with Google, Apple, or another supported provider.
  • Username, avatar, language, region, and account preferences where the app offers personalization.

2.2 Gameplay and app activity

  • Quiz answers, scores, streaks, completed packs, unlocked difficulty levels, badges, ranking state, and leaderboard entries.
  • Feature interactions, session activity, app settings, selected categories, and progress synchronization data.
  • Support messages, bug reports, feedback, and privacy requests submitted to Ronaillabs.

2.3 Advertising, diagnostics, and device data

  • Advertising identifier or similar identifiers where allowed by platform settings and consent rules.
  • Approximate location, device model, operating system, app version, language, crash logs, performance information, and install/update metadata.
  • Ad impressions, ad clicks, rewarded ad events, attribution, and anti-fraud signals where ads are used.

3. Why we use this data

  • Core gameplay: score quizzes, save progress, unlock levels, show results, and maintain leaderboards.
  • Account continuity: support sign-in, restore progress, sync leaderboard identity, and recover user state.
  • Personalization: remember language, category, difficulty, and app preferences.
  • Advertising: show ads, measure ad delivery, reduce fraud, and support rewarded ad flows where available.
  • Reliability: detect crashes, debug issues, measure performance, and improve app quality.
  • Compliance and support: respond to requests, maintain app-store privacy disclosures, and meet legal obligations.

4. Ads, TV metadata, and third-party services

The app may use advertising networks, analytics tools, authentication providers, cloud databases, crash diagnostics, app-store services, and third-party content or metadata providers. TV-related names, artwork, summaries, or metadata may be used for quiz context where allowed by the provider terms or applicable law.

Advertising providers may process identifiers, approximate location, app activity, ad interaction data, and diagnostics according to user settings, consent choices, and provider policies.

5. Google Play and App Store disclosures

Ronaillabs maintains this page to support Google Play Data safety and Apple App Privacy summaries. Depending on the active app version, store disclosures may include contact information, identifiers, user content, app activity, diagnostics, approximate location, purchases, and advertising data.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by service providers that support authentication, cloud storage, leaderboards, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, app distribution, support, and fraud prevention.

7. Retention

Account and leaderboard data may be retained while an account is active or as needed to provide app features. Local progress may remain on the device until cleared or the app is removed. Advertising, diagnostics, and platform data follow the retention practices of the relevant provider. Support messages may be retained as needed to resolve requests and maintain compliance records.

8. User choices and deletion

  • Users can sign out or delete local app data through app or device settings where available.
  • Users can request account or leaderboard data deletion through the Account Deletion page.
  • Users can manage ad personalization and tracking permissions through device, Google, or Apple privacy settings.

9. Children and age suitability

The app is intended for the age groups shown in the app-store listing. Ronaillabs does not knowingly collect personal information from children below the age required by applicable law without appropriate authorization.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when quiz features, service providers, ads, account features, or platform requirements change.
