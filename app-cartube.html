CarTube: Car Screen Mirror

CarTube brings your own videos, supported stream URLs, Picture-in-Picture, AirPlay and GPS dials together in an iOS app.

Bundle ID: com.ronailabs.carscreenmirror. Languages: English, Turkish, Arabic (RTL), Spanish and French.

Choose your experience

Classic speed dial is free. Premium removes app banner ads and unlocks Sport, Runway and Minimal speed dials. Monthly, yearly and lifetime purchase options are shown with localized App Store prices inside the app.

Compatibility and safe use

CarPlay features require compatible iOS and vehicle support. Video playback is only available where supported and permitted while parked. Full iPhone screen mirroring to CarPlay is not currently provided; the screen-capture feature is an experimental local preview. Premium does not bypass these limitations. CarTube supplies no channels or streaming subscription. Use video and setup controls only while parked.

Purchases and privacy requests

Use Restore purchases in the app to recover eligible Premium purchases. Manage or cancel subscriptions with Apple. For privacy requests email support and specify CarTube. The app has no separate user account.
