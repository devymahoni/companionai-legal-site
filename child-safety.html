Lunara AI: Chat & Roleplay — Child Safety Standards

This page describes the child safety standards for Lunara AI: Chat & Roleplay, a Ronaillabs application.

Ronaillabs prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in its products and services.

1. Overview

Ronaillabs is committed to maintaining a safe experience and taking appropriate action against content or behavior involving child sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming, sextortion, trafficking, or other conduct that endangers children.

For these standards, a child or minor is anyone under 18. These rules apply to user content, AI characters, character descriptions, prompts, roleplay, conversations, and AI-generated text or imagery, including fictional or simulated depictions of minors. Using AI does not create an exception to these prohibitions.

2. Published safety standards

Users may not create, request, upload, share, promote, distribute, or encourage any content or behavior that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children. This includes, without limitation:

  • child sexual abuse material (CSAM);
  • sexualization of minors;
  • grooming or attempts to establish sexual contact with a minor;
  • sextortion, coercion, or blackmail involving minors;
  • trafficking, solicitation, or facilitation of child sexual exploitation;
  • any attempt to evade moderation, reporting, or safety enforcement related to child safety.

3. Reporting child safety concerns

Users can report child safety concerns through the support or reporting options available in the app. Reports can also be sent to Ronaillabs by email at yucendm@gmail.com.

Reports should include a description of the concern and relevant account, character, conversation, or message identifiers and timestamps where available. Do not attach, copy, forward, or redistribute suspected CSAM, including screenshots containing abuse material.

4. Action on CSAM and CSAE

When Ronaillabs becomes aware of CSAM or CSAE-related content or behavior, Ronaillabs reviews the concern and takes appropriate action under these standards and applicable law. When we obtain actual knowledge of CSAM in the app, we remove it or disable access to it. Other measures may include restricting or terminating accounts and preserving relevant information where legally required or appropriate. We report to the relevant regional or national authority or legally designated reporting organization as required by applicable law.

5. Legal compliance

Ronaillabs works to comply with applicable child safety laws and regulations. We report CSAM and child exploitation to the relevant regional or national authorities or legally designated reporting organizations when required by applicable law, and cooperate with lawful requests from those authorities.

6. Child safety point of contact

For child safety, CSAE, or CSAM-related inquiries, contact Ronaillabs at:

  • Email: yucendm@gmail.com
  • Child safety support: support.ronailabs@gmail.com
  • Developer: Ronaillabs
