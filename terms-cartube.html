2. Features and compatibility

CarTube plays your selected videos and supported stream URLs, offers Picture-in-Picture and AirPlay where supported, and includes GPS dials and route features. You must supply your own lawfully accessible content. CarTube does not supply TV channels, IPTV subscriptions or third-party media rights.

CarPlay availability depends on your iOS version, vehicle, supported app category and enabled capabilities. Premium does not unlock unsupported vehicle capabilities, DRM-protected content or bypass Apple/vehicle restrictions. Navigation and GPS readings may be incomplete or inaccurate.

3. Free and Premium

The Classic speed dial is free. Premium removes CarTube banner ads and unlocks Sport, Runway and Minimal speed dial styles. This does not remove ads placed inside a stream by a third-party provider. Other features remain subject to the compatibility and permissions described above.
